Funny Memes Wallpaper HD — Privacy Policy

Developer: Maksim Sokolov. Updated: October 8, 2026.

This policy applies to the iPhone and iPad app Funny Memes Wallpaper HD (com.skmlproduct.memeswallpaper).

Wallpapers, favorites and Photos

No account is required. Favorites, downloaded images and catalog caches are stored on your device. We do not upload your favorites or your photo library. Photos permission is requested only when you choose to save an image. The app uses add-only access and does not read your existing photos. Sharing uses Apple's share sheet; the destination you choose handles the shared image under its own policy.

Network requests

The app includes an offline wallpaper collection. It can request catalog updates from GitHub and additional images from image hosts. Pinterest images can be delivered through a Cloudflare proxy operated by the developer. These services receive technical request information, including an IP address, requested resource and connection metadata, and may keep access logs for service operation and security. Favorites are not sent with these requests.

Advertising

The app uses CAS.AI advertising mediation with Google Mobile Ads, AppLovin, Unity Ads, Mintegral and Yandex/Yango advertising SDKs, with related ironSource and AppMetrica components supplied by the mediation dependencies. Depending on consent, platform restrictions and the ad provider, advertising services may process device and advertising identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, ad views and interactions, device/connection characteristics, and diagnostic or performance information. Advertising data can be associated with device identifiers and used for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and analytics. Providers' disclosures can also cover assigned user identifiers, purchase tendencies and other usage or advertising-related data. This does not mean the app offers purchases or collects your payment details. The app does not request GPS/location permission, collect account information or make in-app purchases.

Cross-app tracking and access to the advertising identifier require your App Tracking Transparency permission where applicable. You can refuse tracking and continue to use the app. A regional consent form may also be shown by CAS. You can revisit advertising consent from Settings → Advertising choices and manage tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Read the providers' policies: CAS.AI, Google, AppLovin, Unity, Mintegral, Yandex.

Retention and your choices

Remove favorites in the app and clear downloaded image caches from Settings. Uninstalling the app removes its local data; images already saved in Photos remain until you delete them in Photos. Network and advertising providers apply their own retention periods. Contact us about access, deletion, correction, consent withdrawal or other privacy requests. If a request concerns an advertising provider, we can direct you to that provider. Do not send sensitive information unnecessarily.

Children and changes

This app is an entertainment wallpaper collection for a general audience and is not designed as a children's app. Advertising permissions and regional consent remain subject to platform and provider restrictions. We may update this policy when the app or service changes; the date above identifies the current version.
